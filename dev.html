HLS player. It plays test-media/hls/master.m3u8. To test another stream, add ?hls= and the stream address to this page's address. Add ?mode=hls.js or ?mode=native to choose how HLS plays. Without it, Safari uses its own HLS, and other browsers use hls.js.

Test options: ?debug shows the video size in the VR panel title. ?big keeps the hidden video element large while it plays. Join options with &, for example ?debug&big.
