VR180 Web Player

This is a web-based player for 180° stereoscopic video.

HLS streaming

The same video, streamed with HLS (the first 60 seconds). In a headset, it plays in 3D in 8K, 6K, or 4K. On other devices, it plays a smaller 2D video with only the left-eye picture, in 3K, 2K, or 1K. Choose the quality with the quality button in the controls.
